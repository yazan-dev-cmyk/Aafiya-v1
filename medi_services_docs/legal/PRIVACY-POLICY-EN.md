# Official Privacy Policy (Engineering Compliance Draft)
## MEDISERVICES — OFFICIAL PRIVACY POLICY (ENGLISH DRAFT)

> **Notice:** Engineering compliance draft subject to formal legal review before public deployment (`LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED`).

---

### 1. Introduction & Commitment
MediServices is committed to protecting patient privacy and medical confidentiality. This policy outlines how personal and clinical data are collected, processed, and safeguarded across all platform stakeholders (Patients, Doctors, Clinics, Booking Centers, Diagnostic & Radiology Centers).

---

### 2. Information Collected
1. **Account Information:** Name, email address, phone number, and professional role.
2. **Clinical & Health Data:** Appointments, clinical visits, digital prescriptions, laboratory results, and imaging reports (processed with patient consent under treating physician care).
3. **Technical Telemetry:** Essential encrypted session tokens (`medi_session_token`) and technical security logs.

---

### 3. Purpose of Processing
* Coordination and confirmation of medical appointments.
* Secure management of Electronic Health Records (EHR) and clinical documentation.
* Patient access to prescription QR verification and diagnostic reports.
* Platform integrity, audit compliance, and unauthorized access prevention.

---

### 4. Data Protection & Security
* **Zero Commercial Selling:** Health and personal data are never sold or rented to third parties.
* **Confidential Access Scoping:** Clinical data is accessible strictly to treating healthcare providers and the patient.
* **Encryption Standards:** Full TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, hashed credentials, and immutable access logging.

---

### 5. User Rights & Inquiries
Users may access, inspect, or request correction of their administrative data by contacting: `privacy@mediservices.dz`.
